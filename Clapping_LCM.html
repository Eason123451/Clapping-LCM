Teacher guide

1. Split the class into two groups. Begin counting at beat 1; each group claps only on multiples of its interval. Beat 0 is a starting position, not a clap.

2. Keep the answer hidden. Invite predictions and ask students to clap along with the visual counter. Enable sound for a metronome and two distinct synthesized clap cues. No microphone or recording is used.

3. Pause at the first shared beat. Ask: “Which multiples appeared in both groups?” Reveal the explanation, then connect the pattern to written multiples and equivalent fractions.

4. Explore 4 & 6, 2 & 8 or your own pair. Changing an interval resets the activity. Each interval is 1–12; the timeline shows 24 beats at a time. The activity stops at beat 360.
